PRINT ADVICE — IMG_6150.jpg (READ-ONLY INSPECTION)
- Verified 5252 × 2954 pixels, 15.51 MP, approximately 16:9, with embedded
  sRGB IEC61966-2.1. No EXIF orientation override. No edit, export, portfolio
  selection, move, or upload was made.
- Pixel dimensions support about 17.5 × 9.85 inches at 300 ppi, 21.9 × 12.3 at
  240 ppi, or 26.3 × 14.8 at 200 ppi. These are arithmetic dimensions, not a
  guarantee of sharpness or print quality. A reduced preview cannot establish
  full-resolution focus or noise quality.
- Start with a modest proof: roughly 12 × 6.75 inches, centered with borders on
  a larger sheet to retain the full frame. Inspect the original at 100% before
  ordering. Check separation in the dark foreground/mountain layers and the
  smooth pale sky under the light where the print will hang.
- If the proof holds up, 16 × 9 inches is a conservative next step (~328 ppi).
  Ask the lab for its preferred file format/profile and available paper; a
  matte/lustre comparison can help decide what suits the photograph. No lab or
  print product was selected, and this file is not declared print-ready.

  https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/print/printing-basics-in-photoshop.html
