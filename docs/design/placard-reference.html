implementation notes

  • Frame: a 1px neutral border, square corners, no shadow; 20px padding on small screens and 24px on desktop. Use 24px between cards and 48px between groups.
  • Hierarchy: small uppercase category, 1.4rem regular serif title, .875rem italic subtitle, muted description, .75rem action. Group headings belong above the cards, with one brief introduction.
  • Image: contain the full figure in a 224px-high area. Leave unchosen artwork blank on desktop and collapse that empty area on mobile. Avoid captions below preview images.
  • Interaction: only the linked frame, title, subtitle, and action highlight. Descriptions, category labels, and images remain unchanged. Mirror hover with keyboard focus; respect reduced motion.
  • Semantics: one anchor per linked card; never nest links within it. Use an article or section for static and contact cards. Give real images descriptive alt text. Coming-soon cards have no fake link or tab stop.
  • Responsive: image left/text right at desktop widths; stack image above text below 1024px. Contact cards sit in two columns at desktop widths and stack on smaller screens.
  • Color: olive #6f8200 on white; #b0bf59 on the dark #0c0a09 surface. Keep secondary text and borders neutral.
